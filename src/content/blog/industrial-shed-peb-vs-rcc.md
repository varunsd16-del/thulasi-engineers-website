## Industrial Infrastructure in Trichy's Manufacturing Belt

With the growth of the **Thuvakudi** and **BHEL** industrial zones, factory owners are faced with a critical choice: PEB or RCC? At Thulasi Engineers, we specialize in both, but each has its place.

### 1. Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)

PEB systems are the modern standard for warehouses. They offer:

- **Speed:** Rapid assembly of factory-fabricated components.
- **Span:** Clear spans of up to 60 meters without internal columns.
- **Cost:** Reduced foundation requirements due to lighter weight.

### 2. Traditional RCC Structures

RCC is still the preferred choice for:

- Multi-story industrial units.
- Environments with extreme corrosive elements.
- Projects where local labor and materials are preferred over steel imports.

### Comparison Table

| Feature | PEB | RCC |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Construction Time | Fast (3-4 Months) | Slow (8-12 Months) |
| Clear Span | High (Up to 60m) | Low (Max 15-20m) |
| Foundation Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Fire Resistance | Moderate (Requires Coating) | High |
